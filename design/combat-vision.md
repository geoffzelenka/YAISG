This documnet covers the combat vision for YAISG.

Elevator pitch:
Combat turns time and consumables (potions, food, arrows, etc) into combat skill xp and monster drops, maintaining the other economic loops.  The outcome of combat cycles will be a contest of player stats, equipment, and bonuses against the combatants stats.  Combat cycles will result in damage dealt to one party's hit points until those hit points are exhausted.  

---

Combat loop:

Player chooses to engage in combat at a realm location (inn basement, hunting grounds, deep cave, bandit hideout, etc), player then chooses a combatant to engage in combat with.
  1. Combat cycle starts - based on weapon speed and any possible modifiers
  2. Combat cycle stops: 
    a. Resolve who hit whom and how much damage to deal out
    b. skill xp is earned if the player did damage proportional to total possible xp from the monster
    c. If the player can, consume possible consumables
      i. Health consumables selected by player - or auto triggered by an estate upgrade if available
     ii. Combat modifying consumables selected by player - or auto triggered by estate upgrades if available
  3. if the combatatant runs out of hitpoints
    a. award the player loot from the combatants loot table
    b. wait for a respawn timer and then start combat cycles against a new combatant 
  4. if the player runs out of hitpoints, the player then becomes idle
  
---

Combat-Related Skills - 
Melee, Ranged, Magic, Accuracy, Defense, Vigor

Accuracy + Melee or Ranged or Magic are used to determine combat hits
Melee or Ranged or Magic are used to determine how much damage a successful hit inflicts
Defense + Melee or Ranged or Magic are used to determine if an opponent lands a hit
Defense is also used to mitigate hit damage
Vigor determines the players health points

Combat skills also follow the tier system and have a larger multiplier upon reaching that tier threshold.

---

Player Equipment:
Players will need a number of equipment slots:
1. Standard Equipment: head, chest, gloves, legs, feet
2. Jewelry: neck, left and right arm, left and right ring, 
3. belt
4. Cape 
5. Weapons, left and right hand - some weapons will take up both hands
6. Combat utility slot - ammunition for ranged, weapon oils for melee,  components or something for magic 


Weapons:
3 types - Melee, Ranged, Magic
1. Melee - one hand and shield or 2 handed weapons.  Weapons can have piercing (knives, spears, rapiers), bludgeoning (maces, hammers, clubs, etc), or slashing (swords, axes, etc)
2. Ranged - 2 handed ranged weapons (bow, crossbow, etc)
 a. requires ammunition
 b. if ammunition runs out, combat doesn't stop - but it becomes nearly impossible to do damage
3. Magic - one handed wand and focus or a 2 handed staff

Weapons will give a modifier to the associated skill used for them, 2 handed weapons will get a larger bonuses at the cost of off-hand defense and slower attack speed.

Standard Equipment
Gives defence when using appropriate weapon (some lower tier armor is general purpose, but higher tier is more specialized for one attack skill) and vigor

Jewelry - can provide various modifiers to any skill, even non-combat

Belts - Holds consumable slots, can also provide some skill benefits

Cape - specialized slot for xp bonuses

Utility slot - consumable slot per type of combat - ammunition provides large bonus for ranged, magic components provide a modest bonus for magic use, and weapon oils that provide a small bonus for melee 

Weapons, standard equipment, jewelry, belts, and capes do not have durability and last forever

combat utility slot items are consumables that are used per combat cycle

Combat locations 
These are all up to the content creator.  Combat locations can be permanent - the forested hunting ground with turkeys, stags, bears, etc or the wasteland orc encampment or the heavily populated inn's basement with rats and spiders.  Some combat locations require keys - either from loot tables or found from exploration.

Permanent combat locations are part of a Realm Type and part of that content description.  

Limited combat locations are unlocked via a key item.  That "key" can be permanent or have limited uses.  The key is specific to the realm.  If a realm's type changes, any key related to that realm deactivates.
