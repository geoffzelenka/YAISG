# Exploration and Keys — Vision Document 

## Elevator pitch
Exploration will consume time and optional consumables (to boost chance of finding things) and the outcome of skill cycles will be xp and "access" to resources for other skills.  Exploration will be more of a prestige skill that can grant the player some notoriety within the community, and as such will take significantly longer to level up.  Rewards from exploration can be realm-wide bonuses to gathering skills (a motherlode was discovered, a giant deer herd discovered), a limited access item to unlock a new resource (a map to a rare ore, map to a monster infested cave), and maybe even unlock access to a whole new realm (player Alice discovered a mountain pass to a new realm!).

## Exploration skill
Skill cycles should be much longer than normal skill cycles and successful discoveries should still be rare.  These are all considerations for the content designer.  Exploration skills should have a small gate of gathering and combat skill levels, with higher tiers of those skills providing adjustments to the loot table.  Discoveries are generative in nature, they essentially magic up a new distinct resource.  So each key is distinct and private access.

## Access types
1. Limited-time, realm-wide bonus to a resource skill speed (Player discovers a new particularly dense place of an existing resource)
2. A "key" to a rare resource, not normally found in the realm.
3. Opening up a whole new realm that was previously inaccessible

## Keys
1. a tradeable item on the player market. the key item should maintain who its discovered was and can be flavored as a map or an actuall key as the content designer wishes
  a. the number of generated keys depends on the resource found and skills of the exploring player
  b. the key allows access to a new limited resource, so the player could perform 500 mining cycles and then the key would deplete.  The gathering key should persist until the resource is depleted, and allow the player to visit that node 1 or more times.
  c. keys that go to a combat area will persist so long as the player is doing a combat session.  Once the player stops their combat session the key is depleted.
  d. Depleted keys don't disappear automatically, they just become a trophy item.
2. Once a player uses the key the first time, the key becomes bound and untradeable.
3. Keys in a particular realm will become depleted if the realm is changed due to realm-change mechanics, but should not effect a player if they are in the middle of using a resource unlocked by a key.



## Engine implications
1. The notion of a limited use resource
2. the notion of an access limited resource
3. A skill cycle providing a realm-wide modifier
4. Items that are bound to a player and untradeable.
5. Items that are "tagged" by a player - Player Bob generated this item and the information is stored with the item.
6. A skill being gated by other skill levels.
