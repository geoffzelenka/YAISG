# YAISG - Realm travel and visitation

## 1. Elevator pitch
There are a number of realms in the world of YAISG, and the player may travel to different realms.  The player would travel to a different realm to try and find one that aligns with the player's goals.

## 2. Travelling
The weight of travelling from one realm to another is determined by the inter-realm edge and the current estate of the player.  Some estates are mobile and will travel with the player (A tent or trader caravan) others will need to be left behind while travelling.  If the player has a fixed estate, they will no longer receive estate bonuses when not in their "home" realm.

## 3. Travel costs
A player with a roaming estate will have item costs related to the upkeep of that estate type (a trader caravan needs extra food, fodder, wood planks) and a speed modifier.  The edge between realms will determine the base time cost and a cost modifier to required goods.

## 4. Impacts of travel
If the player has a fixed-realm estate type, travel away from that realm will cost the player the realm bonuses that estate provides.  Any fixed-realm estate inventory is unavailable to the player.  If the player creates any global market orders, the tax from that order should go to the realm that order originated in.


## 5. Engine effects
1. The engine needs to be handle edge costs and apply modifiers appropriately on inter-realm edges
2. There is a difference between fixed and mobile estates
3. When bonuses are determined, the estate must only apply if its a fixed-realm estate and the player is in the same realm
